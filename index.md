### Ideas

<div style="border: 1px solid var(--teal); border-radius: 8px; padding: 16px; margin-bottom: 20px;">
  <div style="font-weight: 700; color: var(--teal); margin-bottom: 10px;">Combined Idea: Volunteer Coordination</div>
  <div style="padding: 12px; border-left: 4px solid var(--teal); margin-bottom: 12px; background: rgba(0,128,128,0.05);">
    <strong>Homelessness Hotspot</strong> - Whenever a person selects the "volunteer" option, they can see a homelessness hotspot map that shows them the most concentrated homeless areas and places that are in need of the most volunteering. This can organize and direct the volunteers into places of more need rather than just random centers.
  </div>
  <div style="padding: 12px; border-left: 4px solid var(--teal); background: rgba(0,128,128,0.05);">
    <strong>Volunteer Shift Calendar</strong> - It would be easier for volunteers to see which shift slots have already been taken rather than being contacted by a member of the team. If there are fewer spots available, then it creates urgency and more people will want to sign up for that specific time slot.
  </div>
  <div style="margin-top: 10px; font-style: italic;">Homelessness Hotspot and Volunteer Shift Calendar can go together to create one combined idea.</div>
</div>

<div style="border: 1px solid var(--green); border-radius: 8px; padding: 16px; margin-bottom: 20px;">
  <div style="font-weight: 700; color: var(--green); margin-bottom: 10px;">Combined Idea: Giving &amp; Donations</div>
  <div style="padding: 12px; border-left: 4px solid var(--green); margin-bottom: 12px; background: rgba(0,128,0,0.05);">
    <strong>Donations Page</strong> - The only way to donate is through an instagram link in their bio. Adding a donation page to their website makes it easier for people to contribute. The donations don't have to be random numbers either. Adding messages like "$50 = 1 meal + supplies" and "$200 = 1 night hotel stay" helps the donors see the impact their money may be making. It makes them want to contribute more to the cause.
  </div>
  <div style="padding: 12px; border-left: 4px solid var(--green); background: rgba(0,128,0,0.05);">
    <strong>Needs Right Now Page</strong> - Money doesn't have to be the only thing people can donate. Adding a "needs right now" page opens the door to more donation opportunities because not everyone can afford to donate money, but most people that are comfortably settled can afford to donate items like toys, old clothes, and even food. This page can show them which objects are most in need by people in the homeless shelters near them and guide them on how exactly to donate these objects.
  </div>
  <div style="margin-top: 10px; font-style: italic;">Donations Page and Needs Right Now Page can go together to create one combined idea (Needs Right Now can be a part of the donations page).</div>
</div>

<div style="border: 1px solid var(--blue); border-radius: 8px; padding: 16px; margin-bottom: 20px;">
  <div style="font-weight: 700; color: var(--blue); margin-bottom: 10px;">Standalone Idea</div>
  <div style="padding: 12px; border-left: 4px solid var(--blue); background: rgba(0,0,255,0.05);">
    <strong>Service Navigation</strong> - Anytime a homeless person accesses the website, they can click on a "Need Help?" Button. After clicking on the button, the website can show them detailed directions to the nearest homeless shelter plus all the facilities available at the shelter. If they need any extra support, they can scroll down to find the contact information of the outreach team with detailed instructions on how to contact them.
  </div>
</div>

<div style="border: 1px solid var(--orange); border-radius: 8px; padding: 16px; margin-bottom: 20px;">
  <div style="font-weight: 700; color: var(--orange); margin-bottom: 10px;">Standalone Idea</div>
  <div style="padding: 12px; border-left: 4px solid var(--orange); background: rgba(255,165,0,0.05);">
    <strong>Dashboard (Impact Made)</strong> - A dashboard showing stats on how much impact was made in a specific time interval that the viewer can select (Last 30 days, Last one week, and so on). In addition to that, the dashboard can have a graph feature showing how many people they've helped over the last year or so. This shows the impact made by the non-profit rather than just stating random programs.
  </div>
</div>
